## ADR-001: Strict Minimal Industrial Editorial Design Language
- **Context**: The client specified strict visual and architectural constraints to convey unpretentious, heavy-duty civil contracting credibility.
- **Decision**:
  - Warm off-white (`#F4F3EF`) background, eliminating sterile pure white.
  - Deep steel blue (`#1A242F`), steel gray (`#3E4A56`), safety amber (`#D97706`).
  - Zero border-radius (`0px`), zero drop-shadows, zero gradients, zero neo-brutalist cliches or glassmorphism.
  - Headings in `Archivo`, body in `Public Sans`. Inter, Geist, and Space Grotesk banned.
  - 100% custom inline SVGs; no third-party icon libraries.
  - Asymmetric editorial layouts with blueprint-grade linework; zero 3-card rows, zero bento grids.
  - Motion limited to quiet scroll reveals and technical count-up metrics; zero hover animations.
  - Copy contains zero emojis, zero em dashes, and no fabricated reviews.
- **Consequences**: Unmistakable authority, high contrast, zero generic template aesthetics, fast load speed.
